## problem statement
Applying to jobs manually feels laborious and time-consuming but good opportunities are also lost if you don't find them and apply on time. It would be a huge help if an extension of me can search for good opportunities for me and apply on my behalf (or at least notifies me) while i am doing the important work (or i am just asleep).

## workflow
build an ai agent that has my cv, career identity statement and my obsidian vault and it searches for jobs on different portals. When it finds a relevant job, it should tailor my base cv for the job, fill the form according to the respective platform (using playwright mcp maybe) and submit the application. This is a typical workflow but for special companies and special platforms (like linkedin cause it can ban my account), i need to branch out and do the submission myself.

## scope
- build an agent that can do the web search.
- it has my cv and my obsidian vault in context.
- it can modify my latex cv.
- it can fill out forms on diverse set of websites such as job boards and company websites.
- it should apply to jobs which are close matches (e.g 80%).
